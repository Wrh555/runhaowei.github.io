01 · Profile

About me

My current research addresses the efficiency limitations of software-based masking schemes for neural networks. I study hardware circuit design, resource optimization, and side-channel security analysis for efficient and secure neural network inference.

I work at the intersection of neural network security, cryptographic engineering, and hardware design. My current goal is to translate secure software implementations into practical circuits for resource-constrained platforms.

My research asks a simple question: how can a protected neural network remain both secure against physical leakage and efficient enough to run in real hardware?
